FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this website officially affiliated with Fabrizio Romano?

No. This is an independent project and should not be presented as officially affiliated with or endorsed by Fabrizio Romano.

Does the Movie Centre host copyrighted movies?

The Movie Centre is designed for discovery, trailers and legal availability information. Full movie streaming requires the appropriate rights or licenses.

Can I request a movie?

Yes, you can use the support form to send a request. Availability depends on territory, provider and licensing.

Can I report a wrong football story?

Yes. Select Football News or Transfer Centre and include the story title and what information appears incorrect.
